# Growth

catalog-search and pricing both sit with Growth. They are joined at the hip:
almost every search result carries a price, and every price we show has to
match the one checkout will actually charge.

Yuki Mori and Sam Delacroix are the two engineers. There is no engineering
manager at the moment; Yuki reports to the Platform EM on an interim basis.

## What we measure

Search-to-cart conversion, and the rate at which a displayed price disagrees
with the quoted price at checkout. The second number should be zero and
occasionally is not.
