# INC-2062 — stock numbers a day behind

**Severity 3. 2026-06-02, 08:30–13:15 UTC.**

Pricing stopped applying stock-driven markdowns, and the operations dashboard
showed yesterday's stock. Nothing broke; everything was simply a day old.

## What happened

The nightly reconciliation in warehouse-sync did not finish. The WMS had
changed a file layout without telling us and the SFTP transfer produced a short
file, which the job correctly refused to load. As designed, it left the previous
day's numbers in place rather than writing partial ones.

warehouse-sync was affected and was the cause. pricing was affected, because it
reads stock levels from warehouse-sync. reporting was affected too, through the
warehouse load.

## What we changed

The job now fails loudly instead of quietly: a run that does not complete by
04:00 pages the primary rather than waiting for somebody to notice at 08:30. We
also asked the WMS vendor for a schema change notice and were told no.
