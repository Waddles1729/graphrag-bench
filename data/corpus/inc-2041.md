# INC-2041 — checkout conversion drop

**Severity 2. 2026-03-04, 14:12–15:40 UTC.**

Checkout conversion fell by about eleven per cent for ninety minutes. No errors
were served to customers; orders were being accepted, then held.

## What happened

fraud-scoring stopped answering inside its 400ms budget. checkout-api did what
it is designed to do and queued the affected orders for manual review, so the
customer-visible symptom was "your order is being checked" rather than a
failure. The review queue reached eight thousand items before we caught it.

The cause was in fraud-scoring: a feature-store migration left `risk-redis` cold,
and a cold cache costs roughly ninety seconds of latency per warm-up wave. The
waves kept coming because the deploy was rolling.

checkout-api was affected. fraud-scoring was affected and was also the cause.

## What we changed

The cache is now warmed before a fraud-scoring instance reports ready. We did
not change checkout-api's timeout, and we would make the same call again — the
degraded path worked exactly as intended.
