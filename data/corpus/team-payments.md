# Payments

Payments looks after everything that moves money: taking it, recording it, and
billing for it. We are a backend-heavy team and we hold the strictest change
freeze in the company (no deploys after 16:00 on a Friday).

Services under our ownership:

- checkout-api
- ledger
- invoicing

If you are wondering why `pricing` is not on that list — it quotes money but it
does not move it, so it belongs to Growth.
