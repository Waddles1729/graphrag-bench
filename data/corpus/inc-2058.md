# INC-2058 — no SMS for four hours

**Severity 3. 2026-05-19, 03:05–07:20 UTC.**

Every SMS we tried to send was rejected. Email was unaffected, which took us an
embarrassingly long time to establish because the first responder assumed both
channels shared a provider.

## What happened

Twilio rejected our traffic after a credential rotation on their side that we
had not applied. notify queued the messages rather than dropping them, so once
the credential was fixed the backlog drained in about four minutes and nothing
was lost.

notify was affected. Twilio was the cause. invoicing was affected indirectly —
the monthly chase-up run had gone out that morning and the SMS half of it
arrived four hours late — but no invoice was missed.

## What we changed

Credential expiry for every vendor is now alerted on thirty days out. The
assumption that email and SMS share a provider is now contradicted in writing on
the vendors page, which is where the responder had looked first.
