# Data retention

What we keep and for how long. Written by Legal, maintained by Data, and
disagreed with by roughly everybody.

| Class | Retention |
| --- | --- |
| Financial records | Indefinite |
| Order records | Seven years |
| Session and device data | Ninety days |
| Delivery receipts for messages | Thirty days |
| Search queries | Thirty days, then aggregated |

Financial records being indefinite is why the ledger never deletes. Session and
device data being ninety days is why a returning customer sometimes looks new to
a risk decision.

## Deletion requests

A customer deletion request is satisfied by removing personal data and keeping
the financial record with the subject pseudonymised. This is the only place the
two rules above are allowed to interact, and it is handled centrally rather than
by each service.
